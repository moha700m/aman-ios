Aman Al-Ghurfa Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Aman Al-Ghurfa collects no personal data. No accounts, tracking, or ads. Magnetometer, Bluetooth, and checklist marks stay on your device. The camera is used only for live lens-glint assist; no images are uploaded. The app may fetch a public catalog.json from GitHub Pages without sending data about you.
